Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This page explains how information submitted through the UPVebx website is used. It applies to the enquiry form on this website.

Information you provide

If you send an enquiry, we receive your name, business name, phone number, email address, service selection and project message. The form sends this information to the UPVebx Supabase database so we can review and respond to your enquiry.

How information is used

We use enquiry details to understand your project, contact you about it and provide relevant service or pricing information. We do not publish enquiry details or sell them.

Storage and access

Enquiry submissions are stored in the Supabase project configured for this website. The included database schema enables row-level security and allows form submissions without allowing public reads when it is applied to your Supabase project. Access to the project dashboard is controlled by its administrators. Please do not include passwords or sensitive financial information in the message field.

Your choices

You can choose to contact UPVebx through WhatsApp or phone instead of using the form. To ask about an enquiry or request that its details be removed, contact us at +91 8247542849.

Updates

This policy may be updated if the website's data handling changes. The latest update date will be shown at the top of this page.
